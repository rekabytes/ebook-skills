# Participant Completion Checklist and Final Reflection

From *Developing eBooks with AI: Three-Stage Express Workflow* by Munaisyah Abdullah.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] I supplied the exact approved learning outcome.
- [ ] I classified and checked my sources.
- [ ] I approved one whole-book Blueprint and Visual Style Guide.
- [ ] I locked MCEDS v1 as the controlling production design system or documented an explicitly authorised alternative.
- [ ] I developed and froze every required topic.
- [ ] I combined only KEEP DESIGN topics, and only after the Stage 3 permission gate plus Prompt 3.
- [ ] I checked the eBook at topic and whole-book levels.
- [ ] I retained real human and technical test evidence.
- [ ] I approved the exact final release version.

Three prompts shorten the experience. They do not remove educator judgement or evidence.
