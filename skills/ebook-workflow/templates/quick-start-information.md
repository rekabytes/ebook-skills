## Product choice (optional — Stage 1 can recommend)

The choice applies to the complete eBook, not just Topic 1.

| Product | Choose it when |
| --- | --- |
| Teaching module | Learners mainly need explanation, demonstration and supported understanding |
| Activity-centred workbook | Learners mainly need guided practice, response space and evidence of work |
| Hybrid module-workbook | Learners need concise explanation followed by structured practice and feedback |

## Learning-design emphasis (optional — Stage 1 can recommend)

| Style | Best for | Signature look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clean Academic | Formal university use and print | White space, restrained colour, clear hierarchy |
| Colourful Learning Guide | Introductory learners and screen reading | Colour-coded blocks, icons and engaging section identities |
| Visual-First Technical | AI, programming, engineering and complex processes | Whole-to-part diagrams and worked transformations |
| Activity-Centred Workbook | Practice, reflection and evidence | Task cards, checklists, hints and response spaces |
| Hybrid | Most educator-developed eBooks | Explanation, demonstration, practice and feedback |

**Recommended default:** choose Hybrid when learners need both independent explanation and structured practice.

**MCEDS v1 production design lock:** the emphasis above may change learning emphasis (more diagrams, practice or explanation) but must not change the MCEDS v1 page format, palette, typography, topic opener, section-heading motif, block semantics, table system, page-number treatment or footer unless an alternative design system is explicitly authorised.
